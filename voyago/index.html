FAQ

Common questions

Why aren't prices live?

Live partner pricing requires paid APIs, commercial agreements and a backend. This college project stays pure frontend, so prices are seeded demo data.

Where do I actually book?

Click “Book on Platform” and VoyaGo opens the partner homepage/search landing page in a new tab. Final price and availability are confirmed there.

Can I still demonstrate the itinerary USP?

Yes. Save a flight, hotel and cab to My Trips, then simulate a 60-minute flight delay. The existing tested timeline, downstream shift and alert logic remains intact.
